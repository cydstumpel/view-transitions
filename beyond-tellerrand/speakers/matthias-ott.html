The Future of Web Design

Matthias Ott is a designer, developer, and writer based in Stuttgart, Germany. He is passionate about web design, typography, and creating meaningful digital experiences. Matthias writes about design and development on his blog and has worked with clients like Google, Adobe, and Microsoft. He is also a co-founder of the Web Design Day conference.
